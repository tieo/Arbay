Four ideas, not one recolored

H06's construction was right — disc/silhouette, one bold accent, an offset shadow, no floating glyph. Its content wasn't: a lightning bolt is Tasker's idea, not Arbay's. This round keeps the construction and changes what the mark actually says — aggregation, convergence, an archived listing, reach across borders.
